Task placement · offloading simulation
Each task of the panels above is placed on the edge, fog or cloud. Cost = α · (latency / deadline) + (1 − α) · (device energy / worst case). Hard limits: memory, deadline, pinned tasks, storage, and safety (the alert must not depend on the WAN).
Assumed tier speeds: edge 0.05 GFLOPS (ESP32, 150 KB usable memory), fog 5 GFLOPS (20 Mbps link), cloud 50 GFLOPS (5 Mbps uplink). Device energy: 3 mJ per MFLOP computed, 1.5 mJ per KB sent. Change them in the TIERS constants. The outage is simulated only; the real MQTT link is not touched.
